01

Business Questions

Audience growth alone gives an incomplete view of operating performance. This project compares reach with daily activity, paid subscribers and revenue to identify what an operations team should monitor and investigate next.

  1. How did audience, daily activity and monetisation change across 12 quarters?
  2. Did DAU, paid subscribers and revenue grow faster than MAU?
  3. What does the historical competitor context add?
  4. Which operational priorities need further internal evidence?

03

Key Findings

  1. DAU grew 160% versus MAU 83% from Q1 2023 to Q4 2025. DAU/MAU rose from 28.0% to 39.6% (+11.6 pp), indicating deeper aggregate activity.
  2. Paid subscribers grew 154% and quarterly revenue 145%, both faster than MAU. The data supports further review of monetisation drivers.
  3. By Q4 2025, MAU growth moderated to 14% YoY while DAU, paid subscribers and revenue grew 30%, 28% and 35%.
  4. The inherited February/March 2026 snapshots show greater Duolingo digital reach among the three observed brands. This is context only, with unverified historical captures.
04

Operational Priorities

These are proposed priorities requiring authorised internal evidence and controlled evaluation, with free-experience guardrails. No implementation or measured business impact is claimed.

Protect engagement quality

Investigate the drivers behind DAU growth outpacing MAU and monitor whether stronger activity intensity is sustained.

Strengthen premium value communication

Test clearer subscription value messaging and targeted trial opportunities rather than assuming faster revenue growth reflects better conversion.

Balance growth with user control

Maintain configurable engagement settings and monitor user response as activity and monetisation continue to scale.

05

Method

  • 12 quarters: Q1 2023-Q4 2025. Official Duolingo IR / SEC disclosures.
  • Compare DAU/MAU, QoQ, YoY and indexed growth (Q1 2023 = 100).
  • Website: February 2026. Social: March 2026. Historical context only.
  • Source alignment and SQL / Python checks support two final Tableau pages.

Growth rates use disclosed values and rounded presentation. MAU and DAU are quarterly averages, paid subscribers are period-end counts, and revenue covers each quarter.

Calculation and tooling details

SQL formulas were checked through a SQLite compatibility adaptation. Native MySQL execution remains unverified. The raw data, processed CSVs, metric definitions and frozen conclusions are preserved.

Definitions: DAU/MAU measures aggregate activity intensity, growth-rate gaps use percentage points, and growth indexes use Q1 2023 = 100.

06

Limitations

  • Public aggregates cannot measure cohort retention or a free-to-paid funnel.
  • Competitor snapshots have no original dated captures and provide context only.
  • DAU/MAU describes aggregate activity. Paid and revenue growth do not isolate conversion or pricing.
  • Relationships are descriptive. Recommendations have no measured business impact.

Website and social figures come from inherited coursework snapshots. Original dated captures, exact observation dates and social account links are unavailable. They cannot establish market share, conversion, retention or long-term trends.

Company MAU/DAU use Duolingo's own analytics definitions and estimates, without independent measurement by this project.
