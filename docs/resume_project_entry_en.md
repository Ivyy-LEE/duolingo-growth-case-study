# Resume Project Entry

## Duolingo Growth & Marketing Operations Analytics | Tableau · SQL · Python

- Analysed 12 quarters of official Duolingo disclosures (Q1 2023-Q4 2025) to assess whether audience growth was accompanied by stronger engagement and monetisation; used February-March 2026 competitor snapshots as limited context.
- Identified DAU growth of **160%** versus MAU growth of **83%**, with DAU/MAU rising from **28.0% to 39.6% (+11.6 pp)**; highlighted engagement and monetisation as review priorities as audience growth moderated.
- Built and published a **two-page Tableau dashboard** with a bilingual case study; framed three operational priorities: **Protect engagement quality; Strengthen premium value communication; Balance growth with user control**, with proposed reviews and tests subject to internal evidence and free-experience guardrails.
